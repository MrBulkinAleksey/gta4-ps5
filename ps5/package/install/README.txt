GTA IV for PS5 - put your game files here.

  /data/homebrew/PPSA94004/install/Grand Theft Auto IV (USA).iso
  /data/homebrew/PPSA94004/install/TU_1A581VI_000000K000000.0000000000205

The disc is your own Xbox 360 copy of GTA IV (USA) as an .iso, and the
second file is its Title Update 8 (USA, version 0.0.8.5). Open the app and
press X on the Home page: it installs the game from them (about 17 GB free
needed). After that you can delete both.

The app hands the install to a small payload it sends to your payload
loader on port 9021 (etaHEN, elfldr or any other), which writes at the
drive's full speed. With no loader running, the app installs by itself,
which is much slower.

No game files come with this app: you need your own copy of the game.
